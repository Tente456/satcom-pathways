## The dataset

The Satcom Pathways deals tracker holds **24 entries** dated January 2025 to October 2026: 9 from 2025 and 15 from 2026. Each entry comes from a public announcement and links to its source. Only one entry has a disclosed dollar value (Satlyt's $8M seed round), so this review counts deals rather than adding up money.

## By segment

| Segment | Entries |
|---|---|
| LEO broadband | 8 |
| Ground segment | 4 |
| GEO broadband | 3 |
| Direct-to-device | 3 |
| GEO satellite | 2 |
| Backhaul | 2 |
| Space software | 1 |
| Mobile satellite services | 1 |

LEO broadband is the largest group with 8 of 24 entries. It includes Starlink licences (Uganda, Seychelles, DR Congo), Eutelsat OneWeb distribution agreements in Angola and Southern Africa, and Amazon Leo's first African consumer partnership, with Herotel in South Africa.

## By type

Partnerships, capacity agreements, and licence and regulation entries each account for 5 of the 24. Service launches account for 3; contracts and infrastructure for 2 each; funding and government programmes for 1 each.

## Three things the data shows

**1. Direct-to-device has reached signed operator deals.** All three direct-to-device entries are agreements with African mobile operators: Airtel Africa and SpaceX for Starlink Direct to Cell across its 14 markets (December 2025), MTN Zambia's field test with Starlink, described as the first in Africa (March 2026), and AXIAN Telecom (Yas) with AST SpaceMobile (March 2026).

**2. Amazon Leo's African agreements are all with local partners.** Its three agreements are with Vanu for rural mobile sites, Vodacom for cell-site backhaul, and Herotel for consumer broadband in South Africa. A separate entry records Amazon Kuiper Kenya's application for an International Gateway Operator licence.

**3. Nigeria's NigComSat-2 programme moved quickly once approved.** The Federal Executive Council approved NigComSat-2A and 2B on 22 August 2026. Thales Alenia Space announced the NigComSat-2A build contract on 14 September and Hughes announced the JUPITER ground-segment contract on 16 September. No contract values were disclosed.

## Where the countries are

South Africa appears in 4 entries and pan-African deals in 4. Kenya, Nigeria and Angola appear in 3 each.

## Limits

The tracker is built by hand from public announcements, so it under-counts private and undisclosed deals. Counts describe announcement activity, not market size.
