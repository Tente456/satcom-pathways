## Key figures

- **29 countries** had granted Starlink a licence as of May 2026, according to Le360 Afrique's country-by-country tally.
- **16 countries** had been announced as "available from 2026" without a licence in place.
- **South Africa** is not licensed. In May 2026 ICASA said that accepting equity-equivalent investment programmes in place of the 30% historically-disadvantaged ownership rule would require an amendment to the Electronic Communications Act.

## Method

Country lists are taken from Le360 Afrique's May 2026 tally and cross-checked against individual licence announcements where available. Licensing status changes often; check the latest regulator notice before relying on any single country's status.
