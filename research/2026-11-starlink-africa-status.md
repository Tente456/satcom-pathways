## Key figures

- **30 countries** show Starlink as live in the company's own availability data, read on 11 November 2026.
- **15 countries** are listed for a 2026 launch.
- **8 countries** are listed with no service date. South Africa is one of them.
- **Egypt and Morocco** carry no launch listing at all.
- **2 countries** show a conflict. Starlink lists Senegal as live although Senegal's Supreme Court suspended its licence in September 2026, and lists Namibia for 2026 although Namibia's regulator refused it a licence in March 2026.

A listing on Starlink's map is the company's own plan. It is not a licence. The figures in our May brief counted licences from a press tally, so the two sets of numbers measure different things.

## What changed since May

- **Equatorial Guinea**, which our May brief listed as announced for 2026, now shows as live.
- **Senegal**'s Supreme Court suspended Starlink's licence on 7 September 2026 after a challenge by Sonatel. Starlink's map still shows the country as live.
- **Côte d'Ivoire** granted Starlink a provisional licence, announced on 11 June 2026.
- **Uganda** got its first commercial Starlink offer when Paratus launched the service on 24 August 2026, three months after the licence agreement was signed in May.
- **DR Congo** became the first African market for Starlink's satellite-to-phone service. Airtel Africa launched it commercially on 14 August 2026.
- **Lesotho** gained an enterprise reseller when Paratus Lesotho opened on 5 November 2026.
- **South Africa** remains unlicensed. In June 2026 the communications department told Parliament that Starlink had not formally applied.

## Method

Country statuses come from Starlink's public availability file, which the site reads on every refresh. The live status for each country, and any change since, is on the [Africa satellite map](/deals/map/). Regulator decisions come from the Satcom Pathways deals tracker, where every record links to its source. A country listed for 2026 that has not launched by the end of the year will show whatever date Starlink lists next.
